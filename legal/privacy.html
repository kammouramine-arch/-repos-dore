1. Introduction

LifeOS — Your AI Life Coach ("LifeOS", "we", "us", or "our") is an AI-powered personal planning and life-management application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information LifeOS may collect, how we use it, how information may be shared with service providers, how long information is retained, and the choices available to you.

By using LifeOS, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires your consent for a particular processing activity, we will request that consent separately.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect information necessary to create and maintain your account, such as your email address and authentication information.

2.2 Information You Provide to LifeOS

LifeOS may process information that you voluntarily enter into the app in order to provide its planning, organization, personalization, and AI coaching features.

This may include, depending on the features you use:

  • Goals and objectives;
  • Tasks and plans;
  • Notes and personal preferences;
  • Planning information;
  • AI conversations and prompts;
  • Responses and information you provide to personalize LifeOS.
Please do not enter information that you do not want processed by the services required to operate LifeOS. LifeOS is not intended to be used as a secure storage service for highly sensitive information.

2.3 Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a LifeOS subscription, purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and its in-app purchase system.

LifeOS does not receive or store your full payment card number. Apple processes payment information in accordance with Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

We may receive information necessary to determine your subscription status and provide the features associated with the plan you purchased.

2.4 Technical Information

Depending on how you use LifeOS and the technical configuration of the application, certain technical information may be processed to operate, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service. This may include information such as device or application information, technical logs, error information, and information necessary to authenticate requests.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through LifeOS only for purposes connected to providing and improving the service.

These purposes may include:

  • Creating and maintaining your account;
  • Saving and synchronizing your plans, tasks, goals, and preferences;
  • Providing AI-powered planning and coaching features;
  • Personalizing responses and recommendations;
  • Providing subscription features;
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the service;
  • Detecting and resolving technical problems;
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. AI Features and Third-Party AI Services

LifeOS includes AI-powered functionality. When you use an AI feature, information necessary to generate the requested response may be sent to the third-party AI service used by LifeOS.

This may include the text of your prompt, relevant conversation information, and other information necessary to generate a response requested by you.

LifeOS does not use your AI conversations for advertising purposes.

Third-party AI providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices. We require service providers handling LifeOS data to provide appropriate protection for user information.

Where required by applicable law, LifeOS will request your permission before sending personal information to a third-party AI service.

You should avoid including highly sensitive personal information in AI conversations unless you understand and accept that the information may be processed by the AI service required to provide the feature.

5. Service Providers

LifeOS may use carefully selected third-party service providers to operate parts of the application. These providers may process information on our behalf and only for purposes necessary to provide their services.

These services may include infrastructure, authentication, database hosting, AI processing, security, error monitoring, and payment or subscription processing.

Third-party providers that receive personal information are expected to maintain protections that are consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable privacy requirements.

LifeOS does not sell your personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably practicable, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own applicable retention policies and contractual obligations.
