### 2. Music
**Neither video contains music.** No music track's commercial rights could be verified to the brief's standard, and the channel's last two productions (WHAT IF? #001 V2 and WORLD ATLAS #001) were delivered without music at the channel's request. So the soundtrack is narration plus original sound design only. There is nothing to clear and nothing that can trigger a Content ID claim.

### 3. Sound effects
All sound effects are original procedural synthesis from `tools/sfx.py`, owned by the channel. No samples or library recordings were used. Every thunder is synthesised with its own seed, distance and roll pattern, so no thunder file repeats.

The sounds are placed by phrase-keyed cue sheets (`tools/cues_short.py`, `tools/cues_long.py`) and include:
- thunder (close crack and boom, mid-distance, far rolls)
- rain (open air, on a truck roof, on a window)
- gusting wind and a distant storm bed
- fire crackle and an electrical arc
- a stream and night insects
- a period truck engine
- tinnitus after very close strikes, a heartbeat (science section)
- document, stamp, tick and low-impact transitions
