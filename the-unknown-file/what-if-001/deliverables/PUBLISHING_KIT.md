## Description — long (paste-ready)

> It's 11:47 at night. Then, without warning, the Moon disappears.
>
> In the first episode of THE UNKNOWN FILE: WHAT IF?, we follow what science suggests could happen next. We look at California grunion eggs waiting for tides that may never come, coral reefs that rely on moonlight to spawn together, the fish and fishing families that depend on those reefs, nocturnal animals on darker nights, and the long-term wobble of Earth's axis.
>
> This is a hypothetical scenario informed by science. The characters are fictional. On-screen tags show what is established science, what is a plausible consequence, and what is uncertain.
