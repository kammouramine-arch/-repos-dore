> On the night of 21 August 1986, a cloud of carbon dioxide rose from Lake Nyos, a small crater lake in north-western Cameroon. ## Upload notes

- Upload the long master at 3840 × 2160 even though it is a 24 fps documentary. 4K uploads get YouTube's higher-bitrate encodes, which keep the grain and gradients clean.
- When you add music in CapCut, keep the narration bed around −14 LUFS and duck the music about 12–18 dB under speech.
