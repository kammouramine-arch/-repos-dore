01 / The premise

Three people were in the station.
The recorder says four.

Kestrel Point is a four-room atmospheric monitoring station on the north shore of the Isle of Morn. In the winter of 2009 it held three people. It is reached by a thirty-five minute crossing from the mainland, and by nothing else.

On the morning of 4 February, Dr Elin Marchetti was not in the station. A coat and boots were on the rack. The boat was on its mooring. There were no outbound footprints in forty millimetres of fresh snow, and the two remaining occupants were asleep in adjacent rooms.

The station’s instruments had been awake all night — a door sensor, a motion sensor, a power log, a weather mast and a continuous audio recorder. Between 02:41 and 03:38 that recorder captured a chair moving, two pages turning, and Dr Marchetti’s voice saying six words.

The inquiry could not reconcile the recording with the search. It returned no finding and left the file open.
