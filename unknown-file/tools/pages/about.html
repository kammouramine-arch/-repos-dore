About

We make files, not stories.

The Unknown File publishes interactive investigations. You are handed the record — statements, logs, transcripts, photographs — and you work out what happened. Nobody narrates it to you.

How a case is built

Backwards. We write the solution first — the whole mechanism, minute by minute, including the parts nobody will ever see. Then we work out what each instrument in that world would have recorded, and we write those records. Then we decide which of them the investigation would plausibly have obtained.

The result is a file that behaves like a real one. It contains things that do not matter. It contains a witness who lies about something irrelevant. It contains one document that quietly disproves the sentence everybody remembers about the case. And because the solution came first, every clue is genuinely there — we are not able to cheat, because there is nothing to cheat with.

What we will not do

  • We do not sell real tragedies. Paid cases are fiction, always. Real disappearances involve real families, and we are not going to charge people fifteen euros to play detective with them.
  • We do not invent facts about real cases. When our short-form content covers a documented case, everything in it comes from the public record and is sourced on screen. If the record is contested, we say so.
  • We do not accuse anyone. Not by name, not by implication, not by editing. Speculation is labelled speculation.
  • We do not use photographs or documents we have no right to use. Every image on this site was made for it.
  • We do not write unsolvable cases. A mystery you cannot solve is not a puzzle, it is a story with a twist. Both are fine; only one is what we sell.

Where this is going

Case #001 is the first published file. Cases #002 and #003 are written and in production. The plan is a new case at a pace the writing can actually sustain, an archive membership once there are enough files to justify one, and eventually cases that run across more than one sitting and more than one investigator.

If you want to know when a file opens, the contact page has the only list we keep.

Fiction, plainly stated

Every case published under The Unknown File is a work of fiction. Kestrel Point, the Isle of Morn, Ardmair, the Bellhaven, the Caldon Atmospheric Trust, the Ardmair Division and every person named in our files are invented. Any resemblance to real events, places or people is unintended. The full statement is on the fiction and sources page.
