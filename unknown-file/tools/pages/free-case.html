The Last Guest is the tutorial for everything else in this archive. It is short and it is winnable, and it exists to teach you one thing:

Do not accept that something is impossible until you have checked whatever was measuring it.

Every case we publish is built to punish an investigator who takes a timestamp at face value. This one shows you why, in fifteen minutes, using a tide table.
