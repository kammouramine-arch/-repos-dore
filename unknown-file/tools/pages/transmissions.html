Transmissions / The short-form system

Forty seconds is the question.
The file is the answer.

Every clip we publish is one page of a real file, cut off at the detail that does not fit. Nothing is exaggerated to make it land. Nothing is resolved to make it shareable.

01 / The rule

Two labels. Never blurred.

Real case / documented

  • Only cases with a public documentary record.
  • No invented detail, no invented quote, no invented witness.
  • Sources named on screen and in the caption.
  • Where the record is contested, we say it is contested.
  • No accusation against any living person, named or identifiable.
  • Never sold. Documented cases are content, never product.

Fictional case / The Unknown File

  • Written by us, start to finish. Every name invented.
  • Labelled fiction on screen, in the caption, and in the file itself.
  • Built backwards from a solution, so the clue is genuinely there.
  • Leads into a case you can actually investigate.
  • Never presented as something that happened.

This is not a legal formality. A brand built on unexplained events only works if the audience can trust which category they are in — and the moment the two blur, both are worth less.

02 / The anatomy of a transmission

Five beats, in this order.

Beat 01

The frame

A place, a date, a time. Cold, specific, no adjectives. “Case #014. 02:17.”

Beat 02

The facts

Three of them, one per line, each one flat. No music swell, no reaction.

Beat 03

The break

The fourth fact, which cannot coexist with the first three. Said in the same tone.

Beat 04

The hold

One second of nothing. The silence is the hook; a viewer who is confused rewatches.

Beat 05

The invitation

“Would you have noticed?” — then the file, not the answer.

03 / Worked example

One clip, one case, one line between them.

This is the transmission for Case #001. It gives away the hook and none of the mechanism — a viewer who arrives from it still has three hours of work ahead.

The clip never states the solution, never implies it happened, and never asks anyone to guess in the comments before the file exists.

On screen · fictional case

In February 2009, three people were alone on an island research station.

At 02:41, the door opened for eight seconds.

At 03:38, it opened again.

Between those two events, the recorder in the common room captured a chair moving, two pages turning, and a woman’s voice.

She had already been gone for an hour.

Caption

Fictional case — The Unknown File, Case #001. Think you can close it? Full file in bio. #unsolved #mystery #detective

Where it lands

Bio link → the free case, not the paid one. A viewer who has never bought anything from us gets fifteen minutes of the real product before we ask for anything.

04 / The funnel

Curiosity, then proof, then payment.

01

Content

Short-form video. One unresolved detail per clip.

02

Website

The archive. Twelve files, two open, the format explained in ten seconds.

03

Free case

Fifteen minutes of the actual product. No account, no email, no card.

04

Paid case

€14.99, or €34.99 for the first three. One payment, permanent access.

05

More cases

A new file on a schedule the audience can rely on, and eventually the archive as a membership.

The free case is the whole conversion argument. If it does not hold a stranger for fifteen minutes, no amount of copy on this page will sell them the long one.
