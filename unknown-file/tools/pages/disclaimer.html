Fiction & sources

The single most important page on this site.

1. Every case we sell is fiction

All case files published under The Unknown File — including Case #000 “The Last Guest” and Case #001 “The Hollow Hour” — are original works of fiction written for this brand.

The people, vessels, organisations, agencies, islands, towns, buildings, documents, recordings and records in them are invented. This includes, without limitation: Kestrel Point Field Station, the Isle of Morn, Ardmair, Ardmair Sound, Sannick, the Bellhaven Guest House, the Caldon Atmospheric Trust, Ardmair Marine Services, the Ardmair Division, MV Sgurr, and every named individual.

Any resemblance to real events, real places or real people, living or dead, is unintended. Nothing in a case file is a claim about anything that happened.

2. We do not sell real tragedies

We do not build paid products around real disappearances, real deaths or real crimes. Those events involve real families. Turning them into a puzzle someone pays to solve is not something we are willing to do, and it is a permanent rule rather than a current position.

3. Documented cases in our short-form content

Our short-form video sometimes discusses documented real-world cases. When it does:

  • The clip is labelled REAL CASE / DOCUMENTED on screen and in the caption.
  • Everything stated comes from the public documentary record. We do not invent details, quotes, witnesses, timings or objects.
  • Sources are named on screen and in the caption.
  • Where the record is contested or incomplete, we say so rather than choosing the more dramatic version.
  • Speculation is identified as speculation, in the same sentence.
  • We make no accusation against any person, named or identifiable, and we do not imply guilt through editing, juxtaposition or omission.
  • Documented cases are never sold and never converted into paid products.

4. Corrections

If we have published something inaccurate about a documented case, or something that identifies or distresses a person connected to one, write to contact@theunknownfile.com. We will correct it or take it down. Requests from people connected to a case are dealt with before anything else in the queue.

5. Images and documents

Every image, plan, chart, photograph-style plate and document on this site and inside our case files was created for this project. We do not use photographs, records or documents relating to real cases, and we do not use third-party imagery we have no right to use.

6. Not advice, not a record

Nothing on this site is legal, historical, investigative or forensic advice, and no case file should be treated as a record of anything. The procedures, instruments and institutions described in our fiction are shaped to serve a puzzle, not to describe how any real process works.

7. Content

Our cases deal with disappearance and with the aftermath of unexplained events. They contain no graphic violence and no gore. They are written for adults and older teenagers.
