Legal / Privacy

Privacy

Short version: your investigation never leaves your device.

Before you publish: this notice describes the site as it is built today — no analytics, no tracking, and nothing loaded from another domain. It must be updated the moment you connect a payment provider, an email list or an analytics tool, and the controller’s legal identity must be inserted below.

1. Controller

The controller is operator legal name. Contact: contact@theunknownfile.com.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you do inside a case — which exhibits you have opened, what you flagged, your notes, your cross-references, your theory — is stored in your browser’s local storage. It is not transmitted to us, we have no copy of it, and we cannot read it.

This also means we cannot recover it. Clearing your browser data deletes it. Use the export button on My Case Files if you want a backup.

3. What we collect when you buy

Your email address, so that we can send your access link and answer you about your order. Your name, if you give it. A record of what you bought and when.

Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach us.

Legal basis: performance of the contract you entered into.

4. Email about new cases

Only if you tick the box. One message when a case opens, nothing else, and an unsubscribe link in every one. Legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Analytics

This site currently runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no third-party tracking. If that changes, this page will change first, and anything requiring consent will ask for it before it loads.

6. Third parties

This site loads nothing from another domain. Typefaces, images and scripts are all served from our own origin, so no third party sees your IP address when you read a page. The only third party involved at all is the payment provider, and only at the moment you pay.

7. How long we keep things

Order records for as long as tax law requires. Email addresses on the release list until you unsubscribe. Support correspondence for two years.

8. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Write to contact@theunknownfile.com. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

9. Cookies

We set no cookies. Local storage is used for your case progress, as described above; it is not used to identify or track you, and it never leaves your browser.
