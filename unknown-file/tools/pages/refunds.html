Legal / Refunds

Refund policy

Fourteen days. No reasoning required. One condition.

The policy

If you have bought a case and do not want it, ask for your money back within fourteen days of purchase and you will get it — provided you have not opened the conclusion of that case.

You do not have to explain yourself. “It wasn’t for me” is a complete answer.

Why the conclusion is the line

A case is consumed at the moment you read the reveal. Everything before that is you deciding whether the product is what you wanted; everything after is you having had it. Drawing the line there lets us be generous before it and firm after it, and it is the only line we can draw honestly.

You can open every exhibit, use every cross-reference, file a theory, and still get a refund. Filing a theory opens the conclusion, so if you think you might want a refund, ask before you file.

If something is broken

If a fault on our side stops you completing a case, you get a refund whether or not you have seen the conclusion, and you do not have to ask twice. Tell us the case number and what happened.

Bundles

A bundle can be refunded in full within fourteen days if you have not opened the conclusion of any case in it. If you have opened one conclusion, we will refund the unopened remainder at the single-case price.

How to ask

Email contact@theunknownfile.com with your order reference. Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within five working days.

Your statutory rights

Digital content supplied immediately is normally exempt from the statutory fourteen-day withdrawal right once you have consented to immediate access. This policy is offered voluntarily in addition to, and does not limit, whatever rights you have by law where you live.
