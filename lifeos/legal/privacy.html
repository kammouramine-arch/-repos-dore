Privacy Policy

LifeOS — last updated 28 August 2026

LifeOS is a personal planning assistant. This policy explains what it stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It describes what the app actually does, not what it might do one day.

What LifeOS stores

DataWhy
Email address and account credentialsTo sign you in. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
Goals, tasks, habits, projects, plans and reflectionsThis is the content of the app. It exists so LifeOS can show it back to you.
Life areas and satisfaction ratingsTo derive your progress view.
Conversations with the assistantSo a conversation has memory and the assistant can refer to what you said earlier.
Usage counters and AI cost recordsTo enforce your plan's allowance. These record counts and costs, not content.
Subscription statusTo unlock what you have paid for. Payment itself is handled by Apple; we never see your card.

What LifeOS does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers. LifeOS does not use the IDFA and shows no ads.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, to anyone, ever.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or calendar.

Who can see your data

You can. Every table is protected by row-level security, enforced by the database itself, so one account cannot read another account's rows even if the app asked it to. This is verified by an automated test suite that signs up two real accounts and checks that neither can reach the other's data.

AI processing

When you use the assistant, the relevant part of your LifeOS content is sent to an AI provider so it can answer. Providers act as processors on our behalf: they process the request and return a result. LifeOS does not use paid-tier provider free plans whose terms permit the provider to train on your content.

Some content is treated as more sensitive than the rest — finance planning, personal reflections, and health or fitness information — and is routed only to providers cleared for it. If no cleared provider is available, LifeOS refuses the request rather than sending it elsewhere.

Voice

The microphone is used only while you hold the microphone button. The recording is sent for transcription, the text is returned, and the audio is not stored by LifeOS. If no transcription provider is configured, the microphone is disabled and the app says so rather than pretending to listen.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. LifeOS sends no marketing push notifications.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. Settings → Privacy contains two controls:

  • Export my data — returns everything LifeOS holds about you in a machine-readable file.
  • Delete my account — removes the account and every row belonging to it. This cascades in the database and is not recoverable.

Deleting the app from your device does not delete your account. Use the in-app control, or email us.

Children

LifeOS is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, export, and erase your personal data, and to object to processing. The export and delete controls above cover access, portability and erasure directly. For anything else, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the app links to the current version.
