## THE SIX DECISIONS WAITING ON YOU

Full treatment — confidence and reversibility per decision — in the
[decision brief](./strategy/02-founder-decision-brief.md).

**The thing most founders get backwards:** decision 1 feels small and is nearly
irreversible; decision 2 feels large and is almost free to change.

1. **Collection architecture** — adopt `CHAPTER 001 — ASCENSION`, separating the
   permanent chapter number from the changeable theme? *Recommended: yes.*
2. **First assortment** — heavyweight tee, hoodie, long-sleeve, embroidered cap.
   Sweatpants deferred to Chapter 002 pending a tonal-match sample test.
   *Recommended: yes.*
3. **No-discount policy, published as policy** — a financial requirement as much as a
   brand position. *Recommended: yes.*
4. **Budget** — five scenarios modelled from €500 to €10,000. *Recommended: €2,500,
   spent samples → photography → platform → paid media, in that order.*
5. **Launch window** — early-to-mid November 2026, going deliberately silent through
   Black Friday. *Recommended: yes.*
6. **Country of establishment** — **the one open input that changes real numbers.**
   Determines VAT rate, OSS registration, returns obligations and consumer-law wording.
   The financial model currently assumes EU-established at 21%.

## TWO THINGS THAT NEED ACTING ON THIS WEEK

Both are cheap now and expensive later.

1. **Trademark clearance.** REIGN is a dense field in apparel Class 25 — live
   registrations include REIGN, REIGN SPORT and REIGN APPAREL. GYMREIGN as one word
   appears clear, but a dense field of similar marks in the same class is exactly where
   confusion objections come from. Commission a professional EUIPO clearance search
   **before** any money is spent on a logo. This is a flag, not legal advice.

2. **Domain.** `gymreign.com` was unregistered as of 2026-08-23, confirmed against
   Verisign's authoritative registry. Availability changes daily. Register it and secure
   the matching social handles at the same time.

## THE THREE THINGS THAT CARRY THE BRAND

Compressed from the strategy, as a reminder of what must not drift:

**The chapter system.** Numbered chapters, opened and closed. Graphics from a closed
chapter never return. Time is the only honest scarcity a print-on-demand brand has —
and enforcing the retirement is the whole mechanism. Break it once and it means nothing
forever.

**Radical spec honesty.** GSM, full flat measurements, print method, fulfilment country,
honest care and shrinkage guidance — published where the category publishes adjectives.
It costs nothing, it answers the customer's real fear, and it is very awkward for anyone
who has been lying about proprietary fabric to copy.

**No discounting. Ever.** The margin structure does not survive a 20% code, and the
category's discount habit is what teaches customers that a price is fiction. Access is
offered instead of price: early access, chapter previews, a free-shipping threshold that
raises basket size rather than cutting margin.
