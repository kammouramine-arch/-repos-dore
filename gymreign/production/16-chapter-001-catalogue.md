# 2. WHY EACH WAS SELECTED

**THE TEE — Freestyler.** The only blank in the catalogue combining 240 GSM, 100% organic
cotton, a true black and **XXS–3XL**. That size range is the headline: Printful stocked the
same manufacturer at S–2XL, so routing through Printify **recovers XS and XXS** and finally
closes the sizing gap that has been open since Phase 05.

**THE HOODIE — Slammer 2.0.** 350 GSM, 80/20 ringspun, oversized, tear-away label, true
black. It was the target throughout and it holds up.

**THE JOGGER — Bella+Canvas 4737.** 339 GSM, Airlume combed cotton, described by the
manufacturer as a *"bold 90s-inspired shape… relaxed streetwear fit with a boxy
silhouette."* **It is the best jogger Printify has — and it is not the JH128.** See §4.

**THE CAP — Econscious EC7000.** 271 g/m² 3/1 twill, **unstructured 6-panel**, sewn eyelets,
**self-fabric closure with brass slider and hidden tuck-in**, OCS/GRS certified, and
**embroidery** on front, back and both sides. It beats the AS Colour 1130 benchmark on
construction and closure, and it is the only strong cap in the catalogue that embroiders
rather than prints.

# 4. THE JOGGER GAP — STATED PLAINLY

**Printify does not carry a genuinely oversized/baggy heavyweight jogger.**

Every one of the 26 joggers in the catalogue was scored on silhouette language. **Not one is
described as "oversized" or "baggy."** The best available is "relaxed streetwear fit with a
boxy silhouette."

**The AWDis JH128 — 440 GSM, oversized straight leg, open hem, XS–3XL, Deep Black — is not
in Printify's catalogue.** Only seven AWDis blueprints exist there and JH128 is not among
them. It remains available at Inkthreadable.

**Where 4737 falls short of the benchmark:**

| | AWDis JH128 (Inkthreadable) | **B+C 4737 (selected)** |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | 440 GSM | 339 GSM |
| Silhouette | **Oversized straight leg** | Relaxed streetwear, boxy |
| Hem | **Open hem** | **Elastic leg cuffs** |
| Sizes | XS–3XL | S–3XL |

**The cuff is the real compromise.** 4737 is not tapered, not slim and not athletic-fit — so
it clears your three hard rejections — but it is a cuffed jogger, not an open-hem one.

**This is a founder decision, not mine to make silently:** accept 4737 as the Chapter 001
jogger, or source the JH128 outside Printify and accept a second fulfilment relationship.

## 7.1 Two issues introduced by the publish — both need fixing

**1. Prices are EUR, not USD.** Printify prices were set as `9200 / 16800 / 14100 / 5400`
cents intending USD. **The store currency is EUR, so they landed as €92 / €168 / €141 / €54.**
At ~$1.09/€ that is roughly **8–9% above** the intended figures. Margins are *better* than
modelled, but the ladder is not what was designed and does not match the Phase 01 price
architecture. **Re-price deliberately rather than leave it as an artefact.**

**2. Vendor reads "Printify" on all four.** Shopify's `vendor` field was populated by the
app. **For a premium house this is wrong** — it is customer-visible in many themes, in
search filters and in structured data. **It should read `GYMREIGN`.**

## 7.2 Storefront is password-protected

`onlineStore.passwordProtection.enabled = true`. **The products are live on the Online Store
channel but the storefront is not publicly reachable**, so nothing is exposed to customers
yet. That is the correct state for a pre-launch store and it should stay that way until the
Phase 01 launch-readiness gate is met.
