# 1. THE FINDING THAT OVERTURNS A LOCKED ASSUMPTION

> ## The Stanley/Stella Flyer is REJECTED by your own hard requirement.

Stanley/Stella's official product sheet describes the Flyer as **"Medium Fit"** `[V]`. Its
Printify blueprint carries **no oversized, baggy, relaxed or wide descriptor** — the entire
description is about pockets, cuffs and eyelets `[V]`.

**Under "hard reject: normal/tapered joggers when an oversized silhouette is required", the
Flyer fails.**

This resolves the whole matched-set saga differently than expected. Months of work treated
the Flyer as the ideal jogger blocked only by EU fulfilment. **It was never the right
silhouette.** The archived matched-set target needs amending — the 350 GSM / 80-20 /
3-end-fleece spec was correct, but "Flyer" as the named benchmark garment was not.

# 7. WHAT US-FIRST FULFILMENT COSTS — TO BE VERIFIED

**This is the trade-off the jogger requirement creates, and it needs your judgement.**

| Factor | Status |
|---|---|
| US customers | Domestic shipping, fast, no duty |
| **EU customers** | **Shipped from the US: longer transit, higher cost, and import VAT/duty on delivery** |
| Fulfilment proximity pillar | **Holds for US, breaks for EU** — the exact weakness identified in US competitors |
| Exact shipping cost and time, US → France | **UNVERIFIED** — Printful quotes at checkout |
| Duty and import VAT thresholds into the EU | **UNVERIFIED** — requires the accountant |

**Two structural routes, and this is a founder decision:**

**Route 1 — US-first collection.** All four pieces from US providers, one parcel, matched dye
lots. Accepts worse EU delivery economics until an EU jogger exists.

**Route 2 — split by region.** Tee and cap fulfil EU for EU customers; hoodie and jogger
fulfil US. **Risk: an EU customer ordering a tee and a jogger receives two parcels from two
continents**, with two duty events. I would not recommend this.

# 8. AMENDMENT REQUIRED TO THE ARCHIVE

The archived matched-set specification names the Flyer as its benchmark garment. **That
should be amended**: keep ≈350 GSM, 80/20, 3-end fleece, same manufacturer, matched black —
but **add "oversized/baggy silhouette" as a hard criterion**, and replace the Flyer benchmark
with **Lane Seven Urban LS16001 + LS16006**, which meets every criterion except EU fulfilment.
