## 1. SHORTS — CONFIRMED IN CHAPTER 001

American Apparel 2PQ, blueprint 6173, provider 217, five black variants S–2XL, €60.
Live and published. **They stay.** Nothing in this review changes that.

They carry the **symbol** rather than the wordmark: the front-left-leg print area is 127 mm
wide, and at that width a wordmark would be 15 mm tall — below the point where the counters
hold on a textured pique. The symbol at 38 mm is the correct mark for that panel.

## 3. SOCKS — REJECT, RE-VERIFIED

All **8 sock blueprints**, every composition read from the live catalogue:

| Blueprint | Product | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| 496 | Sublimation Crew Socks (EU) | 70% polyester / **25% cotton** / 5% elastane |
| 452 | Recycled Poly Socks (Tribe) | 58% recycled polyester / 22% nylon / **15% cotton** / 5% elastane |
| 1433 | Sublimation Crew Socks | 60% polyester / 25% nylon / 10% acrylic / 5% spandex |
| 376 | Sublimation Socks | 95% polyester / 5% spandex |
| 2941 | Unisex Padded Crew (DTG) | 86% polyester / 10% nylon / 4% spandex |
| 574 | Cushioned Crew Socks | 73% / 18% / 6% / **3% cotton** |
| 365 | Crew Socks | **100% polyester** |
| 871 | Mid-length Socks | **100% polyester** |

The best available is **496 at 25% cotton, EU-fulfilled** — and it still fails, for a reason
that no composition can fix: **every one of them is a printed sublimation sock.** A premium
sock carries its branding **knitted into the cuff**. A printed logo on a polyester merch sock
is the single clearest signal of a print-on-demand brand, and 452 is additionally sold as
*"one size fits most"*, which is a merch sock, not a fitted athletic one.

**The category stays empty.** Socks and vests both need a knitted or cut-and-sew supply route
with minimums and stock — a Chapter 002 conversation, not a Printify one.

## 4. PRICING — RECOMMENDATION: KEEP L3 UNCHANGED

L3 is live: **TEE €75 · HOODIE €135 · JOGGER €110 · SHORTS €60 · CAP €45**, free shipping at
€120, flat €9.90 below.

Two facts emerged after approval. Neither justifies a change:

**The shorts are cheaper than modelled.** Live base cost is **$21.79**, not the $25.66 quoted
at approval — that figure was the 2XL. At €60 they run **3.20× on product cost and 51.0%
contribution**, comfortably clear of the 2.8× floor. This strengthens the ladder; it does not
require repricing.

**The cap is the only article under the floor**, at 2.73× and 44.4% contribution — recorded as
a deliberate accepted exception when L3 was approved.

**The identity change costs nothing.** Moving the tee, hoodie and cap from a symbol to the
wordmark uses the same print area and the same single placement. Printify prices per
placement, not per artwork. No cost line moves.

### The one optional adjustment, for the founder to decide

| | Now | Option | Effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| CAP | €45 · 2.73× · €16.64 (44.4%) | **€48** · 2.91× · €18.86 (47.2%) | Clears the 2.8× floor, removes the only standing exception |

**I do not recommend taking it.** €45 is a cleaner entry price than €48, the cap's job is to
lift basket value rather than to carry margin, and raising a price three weeks after lowering
it teaches nothing to a store that has not yet opened. The exception is already documented.

**Recommendation: change no prices.**
