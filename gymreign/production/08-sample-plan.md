**These samples test the garment and the decoration process, not the final artwork.** So

technical demands as the real chapter artwork:

| Element | Placement | What it proves |

# 3. THE SAMPLE SCORECARD

## 3.1 Scoring — 100 points

| # | Criterion | Weight | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fabric** — hand, character, opacity | 9 | Handled blind by three people |
| 2 | **GSM** verified against claim | 6 | Weigh a known area, compare to spec |
| 3 | **Fit** and silhouette | 9 | Worn; photographed front, side, back |
| 4 | **Construction** — seams, collar, cuffs, hem | 8 | Inspect and stress each |
| 5 | **Stitching** | 6 | Density, straightness, loose threads |
| 6 | **Colour** — depth and accuracy | 10 | **Daylight and 4000K.** Black must not read charcoal |
| 7 | **Print** — edge, density, hand-feel | 10 | Loupe inspection; hand across the print |
| 8 | **Embroidery** — density, counters, pucker | 7 | Cap; G counter specifically |
| 9 | **Placement** accuracy vs elevation spec | 5 | Measured from hem with a rule |
| 10 | **Comfort** in wear | 6 | Worn a full day including a training session |
| 11 | **Wash** performance | 9 | Three washes at 30°C, line dried |
| 12 | **Shrinkage** | 6 | Measured before and after. **Fail above 5%** |
| 13 | **Durability** | 4 | Abrasion at cuff and hem; print crack check |
| 14 | **Perceived quality** | 8 | Five people, untold it is POD: *"What would you expect to pay?"* |
| 15 | **Photography** performance | 7 | Shot under the Chapter 001 lighting direction |
| **Total** | | **100** | |

**Minimum pass score: 80/100.**

Colour, print and wash carry the heaviest weights because they are the three failures a
customer photographs.

## 3.2 Hard-fail gates — a failure here fails the product regardless of score

> **A product scoring 95/100 that trips any gate below does not launch.** Numerical scores
> average away exactly the defects that destroy a premium claim.

| # | Gate | Fails if |
|---|---|---|
| **H1** | **Major print defect** | Visible banding, cracked or flaking print, feathered edges, misregistration, or print visibly damaged after three washes |
| **H2** | **Major embroidery defect** | Counters filled in, puckering, loose or pulled stitches, thread colour not tonal |
| **H3** | **Bad fit** | Silhouette not as specified, proportions wrong on a body, or shrinkage above 5% |
| **H4** | **Unacceptable fabric** | Below claimed GSM, thin or transparent, rough hand, or pilling after three washes |
| **H5** | **Visible colour problem** | **Black reads charcoal, grey, brown or faded in daylight.** Any colour shift after washing |
| **H6** | **Poor construction** | Loose seams, collar that does not recover, weak or twisted cuffs, visible supplier branding on trims |
| **H7** | **Incorrect placement** | Mark not at the specified elevation from hem, crosses a seam, or differs between the two units of the same product |

**H5 and H7 deserve particular attention.** H5 is the requirement most likely to disqualify
the AS Colour tee. **H7 is the one that tests the elevation system itself** — if two units of
the same product carry the mark at different heights, the measured-placement method is not
being honoured by the provider, and that undermines the mechanism holding the whole
collection together.

## 3.3 Recording

For each unit: photograph as received · measure and record all placements before washing ·
score against §3.1 · check every gate in §3.2 · re-photograph after three washes ·
**compare the two units of each product against each other and record any difference.**

Run-to-run variance is the thing one sample cannot reveal and the reason every critical item
is doubled.
