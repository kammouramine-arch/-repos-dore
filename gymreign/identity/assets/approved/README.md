### How the flat cuts were made, and the proof they are not a redraw

The master's alpha channel is a clean silhouette with the counters open — the R's bowl, the
G's aperture and the crown's notches are all genuinely transparent. The flat cuts are that
exact alpha, filled with one flat colour. Nothing was traced, redrawn or adjusted.

**Verified: the flat silhouette differs from the chrome silhouette by `0` pixels.**

## The rule

> **Chrome for screens. Flat for cloth.**

Gradients and 3D bevels cannot be embroidered and turn to mud in DTG at small sizes. Colour
is adapted **only** for garment contrast — a silver mark is invisible on Bone or Heather Grey.
The silhouette and proportions never change.

## Tested minimum sizes

Rendered at real physical size and resimulated at a 1.2 mm minimum satin width:

| | Result |
|---|---|
| 70 mm stitched | Clean, crown points fully held |
| 55 mm stitched | Good — **recommended embroidery minimum** |
| 45 mm stitched | Crown points beginning to merge |
| 35 mm stitched | Crown lost |
| 25 mm stitched | Illegible |

**The cap runs at 39.6 mm**, below the comfortable stitch threshold — forced by the blank,
whose front embroidery area is only 44.5 mm tall against a square mark. See
