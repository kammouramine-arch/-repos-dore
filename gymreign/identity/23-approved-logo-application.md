## 1. WHAT THE APPROVED MARK IS, AND WHAT THAT MEANS FOR PRODUCTION

A crowned **GR** monogram: interlocked G and R with a five-point crown seated on the G's
shoulder, rendered in **brushed chrome with 3D bevels, specular highlights and cast shadow**,
on white.

As a brand mark it is confident, masculine and unmistakably "reign". As **production
artwork** it has four specific problems. None of them require changing the design — all four
are solved by adding a derivative cut and a usage rule.

| Problem | Why it matters | Fix — usage, not design |
|---|---|---|
| **Gradients and bevels cannot be embroidered.** Embroidery lays flat thread. A chrome gradient becomes a single muddy grey mass and the bevels vanish | The cap is the embroidery product, and embroidery is what makes a cap read as premium rather than printed | Derive a **flat single-colour cut of the identical silhouette** — one thread colour, no bevel, no highlight |
| **Bevel detail is sub-0.3 mm at garment scale.** At a 45 mm chest mark the highlights print as noise, not as metal | DTG on black renders it as a grey smudge with a soft edge — the exact "cheap POD" look to avoid | Same flat cut for all garment application |
| **Silver on light garments has almost no contrast.** The mark is white-to-silver | Half the recommended palette is Bone, Stone and Ash. On those the chrome mark disappears | A **Reign Black flat cut** for all light garments |
| **It is raster, not vector** | Cannot scale for large prints, cannot be cut for woven labels, cannot be embroidery-digitised cleanly | Vectorise the silhouette from the source file |

### The rule that follows

> **Chrome for screens. Flat for cloth.**
>
> The full chrome rendering is the brand's hero asset — website, social, video, packaging,
> lookbook, anywhere gradients reproduce properly. **Every garment application uses the flat
> single-colour cut of the same silhouette.**

This is standard practice for exactly this kind of mark and it is why chrome sportswear logos
still look sharp on a hoodie. It changes nothing about the approved design.

### One thing to check on the file

The crown's outer points are very fine. Below roughly 50 mm those tips will not hold in
embroidery and will break up in DTG on textured fleece. Once I have the file I will measure
the tip width and, if needed, **blunt the tips by a fraction of a millimetre in the flat cut
only** — the chrome master is untouched. If you would rather I not touch even the flat cut,
the alternative is a 55 mm minimum on embroidery, which is large for a cap front.

The hoodie moves to a **large centre-chest mark**. It is the hero piece, it is the only
garment with the surface area to carry the crown at a size where the crown actually reads,
and one bold statement piece across a restrained collection is what makes the restraint
elsewhere look deliberate rather than timid.

### Colour of mark by garment colour

| Garment | Mark |
|---|---|
| Reign Black, Graphite, Navy, Olive | **Bone flat cut** |
| Bone, Stone, Ash | **Reign Black flat cut** |

### Clear space and spacing

Clear space on all four sides = **the height of the crown**. Nothing enters it — no type, no
seam, no zip, no pocket edge, no drawcord.

### What is never done

No chrome/gradient version on any garment. No outline version. No mark on a mid-tone ground
between 35% and 65% luminance. No second external mark on any garment. No back prints in
Chapter 001.

## 3. WHERE THE REV 3 SYSTEM STANDS NOW

The GR interlock is **withdrawn** — the approved crowned GR replaces it. Two pieces of Rev 3
are worth keeping because they support the mark rather than compete with it:

- **The drawn GYMREIGN wordmark.** The approved mark is a symbol with no wordmark; the brand

- **The colour and supporting-type specification** — Archivo, IBM Plex Mono, and the
  Reign Black / Bone / Graphite / Ash palette. **Recommend: keep unchanged.**

If you would rather the whole Rev 3 system go, say so and the wordmark goes with it — but
then the name has to be set in something, and that decision has to be made before the website.

The hero product is the weakest item in the collection on colour, and it is capped at four.
Three options, all costed live:

| At €135 | 2.94× ✓ | **2.53× ✗ below floor** | 4.45× |
| Credentials | Stanley/Stella organic, Fair Wear | — | Sourced Pakistan |

### Recommendation: switch to the ITC Legend and move the hoodie to €150

- **458 GSM is felt the moment it is picked up.** It is 31% heavier than the current hoodie
  and heavier than almost anything in the price band.
- **Antique silver eyelets and silver drawcord tips.** The approved mark is chrome. The
  hardware matches the logo. That is a coherence argument specific to this brand and it is
  not available on either alternative.
- **13 colours** closes the colour gap completely — Black, Bone, Charcoal Heather, Grey
  Heather, Classic Navy and Olive are all in range.
- **XS–3XL** beats S–2XL.
- At **€150** it runs 2.81× and **€57.57 contribution at 46.1%** — back above the floor.

**The honest cost:** it loses the Stanley/Stella organic and Fair Wear credential, which is
a real brand asset and is currently published in the product description. 70/30 also has
more polyester than 80/20.

**Why not the Box Hoodie**, despite being $18 cheaper and allowing a *lower* price: **60/40
cotton**. Publishing "60% cotton, 40% polyester" on a €135 hoodie undercuts the spec-honesty
the brand is built on. It is the cheap option and it reads that way on the label.

**If you will not move the price:** keep the Slammer at €135 and accept three colours. That
is a defensible launch — just say so, and I will stop recommending the swap.

## 7. STOREFRONT DIRECTION

- **Shoot order on the grid: Black first, always.** Black is the brand. Every product card
  shows Reign Black as its default swatch; colour is discovered on the product page.
- **One mockup camera per product, used consistently.** Mixed flat-lay and on-model across a
  five-product grid is the single fastest way to look like a POD store.
- **Swatches as squares, not circles**, in the house order: Black · Bone · Graphite · Ash ·
  Navy · Stone. Same order on every product, every time.
- **No lifestyle photography until it can be shot properly.** An empty, disciplined grid on
  Reign Black reads more expensive than borrowed lifestyle imagery.
- **Collections: one.** "CHAPTER 001 — ASCENSION". Not "T-shirts", "Hoodies", "Accessories".
