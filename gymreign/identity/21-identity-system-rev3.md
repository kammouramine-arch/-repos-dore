# 21 — GYMREIGN IDENTITY SYSTEM (Rev 3) — PROPOSAL

**Status: PROPOSAL. Nothing published. No product, price or store setting changed.**
Issued 2026-08-30 · Supersedes the GR plate mark on approval

---

## 0. WHAT CHANGED AND WHY

The GR plate was a bounded lockup of two letters set inside two rules. It was legible and
it was safe. It was not an identity — it had no alphabet behind it, so the wordmark and the
mark had no relationship, and the wordmark did not exist as artwork at all.

Rev 3 inverts the order of work: **the alphabet is designed first, and the symbol is cut out
of it.** That is why the symbol reads as GYMREIGN's and not as a generic monogram — the G in
the mark and the G in the name are literally the same drawn form.

**24 symbol concepts were built, rendered and tested. 23 were rejected.** The rejection
record is in §9, because the reasons are the useful part.

## 1. THE PRIMARY LOGO — the stacked lockup

`assets/system/gymreign-lockup-stacked.svg`

Symbol above, wordmark below, both centred on a shared vertical axis, separated by 26 units
at a symbol height of 100. This is the default logo wherever there is room for two lines:
site header, packaging, lookbook, print.

## 2. THE SYMBOL — the GR interlock

`assets/system/gymreign-symbol.svg` · **183.7 × 100 units, 1.84 : 1**

G and R, the two halves of the name, interlocked so that **the R's stem occupies the G's
aperture.** They are not two letters placed side by side; the aperture that makes the G a G
is the same space that carries the R's stem, and the G's datum bar runs into the R's stem to
close the join. Remove either letter and the other is incomplete.

**Construction:** stroke 22, cap 100, flat-sided ring at corner radius 30, aperture opening
at 0.72 of the ring's top edge, overlap 22 (one stroke width), R bowl 82 wide, leg at the
house angle of 0.52 run per unit rise.

The symbol is cut at a heavier weight than the wordmark (22 against 20) so that the two
appear optically equal at the sizes each is actually used at.

## 3. THE WORDMARK

`assets/system/gymreign-wordmark.svg` · **808 × 100 units, 8.08 : 1**

Eight glyphs, drawn as outlines. Not a typeface, not a licensed font, not letter-spaced
Archivo. The construction rules are in §11.

## 4. HORIZONTAL LOCKUP

`assets/system/gymreign-lockup-horizontal.svg`

Symbol at full height, wordmark at cap 56, gap 38, wordmark centred on the symbol's vertical

## 5. COMPACT LOCKUP

`assets/system/gymreign-lockup-compact.svg`

## 6. MONOCHROME

The identity is monochrome by construction. There is no colour version, no gradient, no
outline version and no drop shadow. Two renderings only:

| | Mark | Ground |
|---|---|---|
| **Positive** | Bone `#E9E4DA` | Reign Black `#0E0F11` |
| **Negative** | Reign Black `#0E0F11` | Bone `#E9E4DA` |

For single-colour print on any other ground, the mark is 100% of the single ink. Never
tinted, never at reduced opacity.

## 7. LIGHT AND DARK APPLICATION

- **On Reign Black or any ground darker than 35% luminance** — Bone mark.
- **On Bone or any ground lighter than 65% luminance** — Reign Black mark.
- **Between 35% and 65%** — the mark does not go on that ground. Place a Reign Black or Bone
  field first and put the mark on that.
- **On photography** — Bone mark only, and only where the underlying image is below 35%
  luminance across the whole clear-space box, not just behind the strokes.

## 8. EMBROIDERY AND PRINT — TESTED MINIMUMS

Every number below comes from rendering the artwork at the stated physical size and
resimulating it at a 1.2 mm minimum satin width, then reading the result. They are not
estimates.

| | Embroidery min | Embroidery recommended | Print min (DTG/DTF) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Symbol** | **20 mm wide** | 30 mm | **16 mm wide** |
| **Wordmark** | **45 mm wide** | 60 mm | **40 mm wide** |
| **Compact lockup** | not for embroidery | — | 45 mm wide |

**What fails, and where:** the symbol's R counter closes at **14 mm** stitched. The wordmark's
counters merge at **32 mm** stitched and it is illegible at 24 mm. Print resolves detail to
0.4 mm and holds the counters open well below these figures, so the print minimums above are
set by legibility rather than by ink.

**Embroidery specification:** satin stitch, 0.35 mm pull compensation, stroke direction across
the short axis of each stroke. No fill stitch. No outline. The datum bar and the ring are one
continuous stitch path.

## 9. SMALL-SIZE AND CAP VERSION

There is no separate simplified drawing. The symbol was designed against the 20 mm stitched
threshold, so the production artwork is the small-size artwork. **Nothing is redrawn, thinned
or simplified for small use** — a second drawing is a second identity.

For cap fronts, the symbol at 45 mm or the wordmark at 90 mm. Both were tested stitched.

## 10. CLEAR SPACE AND SIZING

**Clear space = 2 stroke widths = 44 units at a symbol height of 100 = 0.44 × symbol height,
on all four sides.** Nothing enters that box: no type, no rule, no image edge, no garment seam,
no zip, no pocket edge.

For the wordmark, clear space is measured off the cap height by the same rule: 0.44 × cap
height on all four sides.

**Sizing on garments** — one mark, one placement, per garment. No back prints. No second
external expression. This stays inside the locked garment rule with one expression to spare.

## 11. TYPOGRAPHY SPECIFICATION

### The GYMREIGN alphabet (proprietary, drawn)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Cap height | 100 units |
| Stroke weight | **20** (wordmark) · **22** (symbol cut) |
| Stroke-to-cap ratio | 0.20 |
| Diagonal angle | **0.52 run per unit rise** — 27.5° from vertical, used on Y, M, R and N without exception |
| Datum band | **y = 42 to 62** |
| Flat-side corner radius | 30 |
| Terminals | cut horizontally at the cap line and baseline; diagonals cut square |
| Counters | rectangular |
| Spacing | optical — every advance set so the tightest ink gap between any two adjacent letters is equal at 19 units |

**The datum band is the alphabet's single organising rule.** The G's bar, the R's bowl
bottom, the E's middle arm, the M's truncated apex and the Y's junction all terminate inside
`y = 42–62`. One horizontal line runs through the whole word. The M is the clearest statement
of it: its V does not reach the baseline — **it stops flat on the datum.**

Measured advances: `GY 97.5 · YM 106.8 · MR 130.8 · RE 131.0 · EI 96.8 · IG 38.8 · GN 114.8`

### Supporting typefaces (unchanged from Rev 2)

| Role | Face | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | **Archivo** SemiBold 600 | Titles, product names, campaign lines |
| Body | **Archivo** Regular 400 | Running copy, 65-character measure |
| Data | **IBM Plex Mono** Medium 500 | Specifications, sizes, prices, chapter numbers, labels. Uppercase, 0.14 em tracking |

The alphabet is never used for running text. It sets the name and nothing else.

## 12. COLOUR SPECIFICATION

Unchanged from Rev 2, and deliberately so: a monochrome palette is the correct answer to a
brief asking for dominance, precision and luxury, and the palette was already locked.

| | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reign Black** | `#0E0F11` | 14 · 15 · 17 | 72 / 63 / 60 / 62 | Primary ground, garment colour, mark on light |
| **Bone** | `#E9E4DA` | 233 · 228 · 218 | 8 / 8 / 13 / 0 | Mark on dark, second garment colour, paper |
| **Graphite** | `#4B4F54` | 75 · 79 · 84 | 63 / 51 / 45 / 15 | Secondary type, rules, disabled states |
| **Ash** | `#8B8F94` | 139 · 143 · 148 | 47 / 37 / 34 / 2 | Captions, metadata |

**Thread:** Reign Black — Madeira Polyneon 1800 or nearest. Bone — Madeira Polyneon 1771 or
nearest. Match to a physical thread card before the first production run; screen values are
not thread values.

**There is no accent colour.** Adding one is the fastest way to make this look like everyone
else.

## 13. THE REJECTION RECORD

24 concepts built and rendered. 23 rejected. Recorded because the reasons are reusable.

| Concept | Rejected because it read as |
|---|---|
| Ascent — datum bar with graduated uprights | a bar chart |
| Step — free-standing ascending blocks | mobile signal bars |
| Gate — two monoliths and a lintel | the letters "IT" |
| Peak — asymmetric chevron on a datum | a tent |
| Aperture — broken ring with an outward bar | a power button |
| Blade — ascending slash off a datum | a tick, and the closest thing on the sheet to an existing brand |
| Overshoot — monolith crossed by a bar | a cross |
| Arch — heavy portal on the datum | a tombstone |
| Standard — notched banner | a bookmark icon |
| Plumb — mass hung from a line | a map pin |
| Benchmark — wedge rising into a bar | the letter Z |
| Stack GR — two letters in a square | a running figure |
| Sovereign R with a left overshoot tab | a pistol — a mid-height left tab plus a right-kicking leg |
| Ligature GR, first attempt | mush; the bowls collided |
| Monolith R alone | competent, but a lone bold R is Represent's territory |
| G alone, four variants | a clean G, and nothing more |
| G with a bolted-on leg, three variants | a G with something stuck to it |
| Knocked-out plate and tab versions | **a solid blob at 15 px** — the letters vanish entirely |

**The pattern:** heavy abstract geometry lands on an existing icon nearly every time. There
are very few unclaimed silhouettes at that weight. Letterform is where the unclaimed ground
is, because a letter can be pushed into strange proportions and still be read.

## 14. HONEST LIMITS

- **No logo is instantly recognisable on day one.** Recognition is bought with consistent
  exposure. What this system can do is be unmistakably distinct from the field, simple enough
  to be learned fast, and rigid enough that every application reinforces the last.
- **The alphabet covers eight glyphs** — G, Y, M, R, E, I, N. It sets the name. A full
  character set does not exist and is not needed until there is a second word to set.
- **The thread colours are screen matches.** They must be confirmed against a physical card.
- **The symbol has not been stitched.** The 20 mm minimum comes from simulation at a 1.2 mm
  satin width, which is the right model, but a physical sew-out on the actual cap before the
  first order would confirm it.
